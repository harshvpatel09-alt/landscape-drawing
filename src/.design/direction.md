# Direction — the sketchbook on the cutting mat

PRODUCT / USER / PRIMARY GOAL
A landscape-drawing course for one adult learner working in graphite. The primary action is always
"draw the next thing": continue the lesson, do the drill, submit the drawing. In five seconds the
learner must see where they are, what to draw next, and one piece of real artwork.

VISUAL DIRECTION
The studio table from the source videos: a navy self-healing cutting mat with a grid, a sheet of
drawing paper on it, graphite pencils graded 4H to 6B, and light construction lines in non-photo blue.
Calm, quiet, tactile. The artwork is the loudest thing on every screen.

HIERARCHY
1. The plate (the drawing or diagram) — largest element on every lesson screen.
2. The single next action (Continue / Next plate / Submit drawing).
3. Lesson prose in a narrow textbook column.
4. Progress and navigation, kept on the mat and at the edges.

LAYOUT
Desktop: mat-coloured sidebar (navigation) + paper workspace. Lesson screens are "plates":
a wide art field on the left, a narrow text column on the right, a sticky footer with Back/Next.
Mobile: plates stack art-first; a 5-item bottom bar replaces the sidebar.

TYPOGRAPHY
Newsreader (serif, optical sizes) for headings and lesson prose: the voice of a drawing textbook.
Schibsted Grotesk for interface text, labels, numbers (tabular). No monospace. Sentence case labels.
Scale: 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 22 / 28 / 36 / 48 / 64.

COLOR (each has one job)
- Paper #F2F3F0 / Sheet #FBFBF9 — the drawing surface; plates stay paper in dark mode.
- Graphite ramp v0–v8 — every value in the artwork and the value lessons (a real 9-step scale).
- Ink #1F2124 — text and the primary button (a 6B mark).
- Mat #1B2433 — navigation surface with a faint 2cm grid; the physical mat from the videos.
- Non-photo blue #3E8FC7 (lines) / #1D5C8C (text-safe) — construction lines, annotations, focus.
- Semantic: good #2E7650, warn #946514, bad #A63A2E — only for feedback states, always with text.

DENSITY / INTERACTION / RESPONSIVE
Comfortable density. One primary action per screen. Targets ≥ 44px. Keyboard reachable.
Every exercise gives immediate, explained feedback.

MOTION
Precise and quiet. One orchestrated moment: demonstration steps "draw in" with stroke reveal,
mirroring watching a pencil demo. UI transitions 140–220ms. Reduced motion shows final states.

IMAGERY
Illustration: all landscapes are procedurally drawn SVG "graphite" plates made for this course,
so every diagram can be annotated, decomposed into steps and converted to 3 values. No stock photos.

SIGNATURE
Progress is shaded in with pencil hatching, and every plate can switch between line, tone and
3-value views, because this course teaches by seeing the same scene in different ways.
